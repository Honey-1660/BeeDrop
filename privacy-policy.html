Privacy Policy

BeeDrop is built and maintained by Sarkar Labs / MS Technologies ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information BeeDrop collects, why, and what rights you have over it. It applies to the BeeDrop website, the Windows and macOS desktop apps, and the Android web app.

1. What BeeDrop Does With Your Files

BeeDrop transfers files directly between your own devices over your local Wi-Fi network, LAN, or mobile hotspot. Your files are never uploaded to, stored on, or routed through any server we operate. File transfer happens peer-to-peer, directly between the sending and receiving device.

2. What Information We Collect

We try to collect as little as possible. Here's exactly what BeeDrop touches:

DataWhat it isWhy we collect itWhere it goes
Local network discovery data Device name, local IP address, and connection type of devices on your network To let BeeDrop find and display nearby devices Stays on your local network — not sent to us
Crash and error reports App version, OS version, device model, and the error that occurred To help us fix bugs and improve stability Sent to our crash-reporting service. No file contents, name, or email included.
Website usage Basic page visit information when you browse our website Standard hosting logs Held by our hosting provider

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or the contents of files you transfer. We do not require account creation to use BeeDrop.

3A. Advertising & Cookies

Our website displays third-party advertisements to support free development and hosting of BeeDrop. To do this, our ad partner (e.g. Google AdSense) may place cookies or similar technologies on your device to:

  • Show ads that may be more relevant to you
  • Measure how ads perform
  • Limit how many times you see the same ad

What this means for you:

  • These cookies are set by our third-party ad partner, not directly by us — their own privacy policy governs how they use this data (for Google: policies.google.com/technologies/ads).
  • You can control or opt out of personalized advertising via Google's Ad Settings.
  • We do not link ad-cookie data to your identity.
  • Ads are shown on our website only. BeeDrop's core file-transfer function does not use ad tracking.

By continuing to use our website with ads enabled, you consent to this use of cookies as described. If you do not consent, you can use a browser ad/tracker blocker, or use the BeeDrop desktop/mobile apps directly, which do not display ads.

4. Your Rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • Know what data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data, where applicable.
  • Request deletion of any data associated with a crash report you can identify.
  • Withdraw consent for crash reporting at any time via app settings or by uninstalling the app.
  • Nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity.

5. Data Retention

Crash and error reports are retained only as long as needed to diagnose and fix issues, and are periodically purged. We do not retain local network discovery data — it exists only in-app, in real time, while BeeDrop is running.

6. Children's Privacy

BeeDrop is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from users under 18. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has used BeeDrop, please contact us so we can address it.

7. Security

Because BeeDrop transfers files directly between devices on your local network rather than through our servers, your files are not exposed to us or to any third party during transfer. We recommend only using BeeDrop on networks you trust.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify users within the app.
