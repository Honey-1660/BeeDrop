# Privacy Policy — BeeDrop

**Last updated:** August 10, 2026

BeeDrop is built and maintained by **Sarkar Labs / MS Technologies** ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information BeeDrop collects, why, and what rights you have over it. It applies to the BeeDrop website (beedrop.netlify.app), the Windows and macOS desktop apps, and the Android web app.

---

## 1. What BeeDrop Does With Your Files

BeeDrop transfers files directly between your own devices over your local Wi-Fi network, LAN, or mobile hotspot. **Your files are never uploaded to, stored on, or routed through any server we operate.** File transfer happens peer-to-peer, directly between the sending and receiving device.

## 2. What Information We Collect

We try to collect as little as possible. Here's exactly what BeeDrop touches:

| Data | What it is | Why we collect it | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Local network discovery data** | Device name, local IP address, and connection type of devices on the same network as you | To let BeeDrop find and display nearby devices so you can send files to them | Stays on your local network — not sent to us |
| **Crash and error reports** | Technical diagnostic information (e.g. app version, operating system version, device model, and the error that occurred) when the app crashes or hits an unexpected error | To help us fix bugs and improve stability | Sent to our crash-reporting service when a crash occurs. We do not intentionally include your name, email, or file contents in these reports. |
| **Website usage** | Basic page visit information if you browse beedrop.netlify.app | Standard hosting logs from our website host (Netlify) | Held by our hosting provider under their own privacy practices |

We do **not** collect your name, email address, phone number, or the contents of files you transfer. We do not require account creation to use BeeDrop.

## 3. Third-Party Services We Use

| Service | Purpose | Data Involved |
|---|---|---|
| Netlify | Hosts our website | Standard web server logs (IP address, browser type) |
| Crash/error reporting service | Helps us detect and fix app crashes | Technical diagnostic data as described above |
| Google AdSense (or similar ad network) | Displays advertising on our website to support free development of BeeDrop | Uses cookies/similar technologies to show relevant ads and measure ad performance. This may include your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and general location. See Section 3A below. |
| GitHub / direct file hosting | Serves our installer files for download | No personal data — used only to serve the download file |

Where any of these providers are based outside India, this involves a cross-border transfer of the limited technical data described above, handled under that provider's own data protection terms.

## 3A. Advertising & Cookies

Our website displays third-party advertisements to support free development and hosting of BeeDrop. To do this, our ad partner (e.g. Google AdSense) may place cookies or similar technologies on your device to:

- Show ads that may be more relevant to you
- Measure how ads perform
- Limit how many times you see the same ad

**What this means for you:**
- These cookies are set by our third-party ad partner, not directly by us — their own privacy policy governs how they use this data (for Google: [policies.google.com/technologies/ads](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)).
- You can control or opt out of personalized advertising through your browser settings or via [Google's Ad Settings](https://adssettings.google.com).
- We do not link ad-cookie data to your identity — we don't know who you are, only that "a visitor" saw an ad.
- Ads are shown on our website only. **BeeDrop's core file-transfer function does not use ad tracking.**

By continuing to use our website with ads enabled, you consent to this use of cookies as described. If you do not consent, you can use a browser ad/tracker blocker, or use the BeeDrop desktop/mobile apps directly, which do not display ads.

## 4. Your Rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

- **Know** what data we hold about you — in BeeDrop's case, this is limited to crash/diagnostic logs, as described above.
- **Correct** inaccurate data, where applicable.
- **Request deletion** of any data associated with a crash report you can identify (contact us using the details below).
- **Withdraw consent** for crash reporting at any time by disabling it in the app settings, where available, or by uninstalling the app.
- **Nominate** another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity, in accordance with the Act. To register a nominee, contact us using the details below.

## 5. Data Retention

Crash and error reports are retained only as long as needed to diagnose and fix issues, and are periodically purged. We do not retain local network discovery data — it exists only in-app, in real time, while BeeDrop is running.

## 6. Children's Privacy

BeeDrop is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from users under 18. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has used BeeDrop and provided data beyond the automatic diagnostics described above, please contact us so we can address it.

## 7. Security

Because BeeDrop transfers files directly between devices on your local network rather than through our servers, your files are not exposed to us or to any third party during transfer. We recommend only using BeeDrop on networks you trust (e.g. your home Wi-Fi or your own hotspot).

## 8. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify users within the app.
